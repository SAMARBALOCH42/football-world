About Football World

More Than Just a Game

Football connects people through passion, teamwork, and unforgettable moments.

Who We Are

Football World is a student-created website designed for people who love football. It brings football-related information together in one place using HTML and CSS.

⚽ Our Mission

To create an attractive and easy-to-use website where fans can explore football teams, players, matches, and stories.

🌍 Our Passion

We celebrate the excitement of football, the creativity of players, and the spirit of teamwork.

🏆 Our Goal

To provide a simple and enjoyable experience for football fans on computers and mobile devices.

What You Can Explore

Visit the Teams page to discover famous clubs, the Players page to read about football stars, and the Gallery page to explore football photography. You can also view sample fixtures and learn about famous trophies.
